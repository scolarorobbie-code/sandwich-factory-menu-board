## 2. Website improvement plan (prioritized)

> Reality check: the current site is an **Orda** product. Some of these fixes are
> easy on the existing site; others are best handled when we replace the
> Orda-powered ordering with the new app + a clean web order page. I've flagged
> which is which.

1. **Make "Order Pickup" the loudest thing on the page.** One big button, above the
   fold, on mobile. Right now ordering lives behind Orda's `/s/order` flow. When
   the new app ships, this button should (a) deep-link to the app if installed,
   (b) offer App Store / Google Play download, and (c) fall back to a web order
   page. _(Best done at app launch.)_
2. **Fix the basics customers need in 5 seconds:** correct hours, a tap-to-call
   button for **(615) 494-1211**, the **116 Chaffin Pl** address with a tap-to-map
   link, and a "we do pickup here / delivery on DoorDash & Uber Eats" line so the
   site doesn't compete with itself. _(Easy now.)_
3. **Real food photos on the menu.** Your best marketing is a great photo of the
   Cuban and the Factory Club. We're already wiring real Square photos into the app
   with AI/stock fallbacks — shoot a dozen good hero photos once and reuse them on
   the site, app, Google, and social. _(One photo session powers everything.)_
4. **Mobile speed.** Orda splash pages can be heavy. Whatever the site is, it must
   load fast on a phone on cell data — most of your traffic is "I'm hungry, near
   Old Fort Pkwy, searching now." _(Verify; address at app launch.)_
5. **Local SEO for "sandwich Murfreesboro" / "Cuban sandwich Murfreesboro" / "best
   sandwich near me."** Put the city + signature items in the page title and
   headings, keep NAP (Name/Address/Phone) identical everywhere, and add
   Restaurant + Menu structured data so Google shows hours and menu in search.
6. **Google Business Profile is your real homepage — optimize it.** Confirm hours,
   add the new "Order Pickup" link as the booking/order action, post weekly
   specials there (Google Posts), and reply to recent reviews. With ~1,129 reviews
   you have huge equity; keep feeding it.
7. **Add the loyalty hook.** A simple "Earn **Stars** on every order in our app"
   banner once the app and Square Loyalty are live, to drive downloads from web
   traffic. _(After loyalty is wired.)_

## 3. Social media plan (per platform)

**Overall strategy:** you don't need to be everywhere. Win **Instagram +
Facebook** (one effort feeds both via cross-posting) and keep **Google** fed.
Skip X/Twitter. TikTok only if someone on staff genuinely enjoys making short
videos.

**Content pillars (rotate these — works for a sandwich shop):**
1. **Hero food close-ups** — Cuban, buffalo chicken, Factory Club, fries.
2. **Behind-the-counter / "made fresh"** — slicing meat, building a sandwich.
3. **Specials & deals** — daily/weekly, and app-exclusive deals once live.
4. **People & local** — staff, regulars, "since 2012," Murfreesboro pride.
5. **Calls to action** — "Order pickup in our app, earn Stars."

### Instagram — [@sandwichfactorytn](https://www.instagram.com/sandwichfactorytn/) — **worth it: yes, top priority**
- **Cadence:** 3 feed posts/week + a few Stories. Reels (short videos of a sandwich
  being made) get the most reach — aim for 1/week.
- **Fix the bio now:** one line of who you are + a link. Today the link should point
  to the website order page; at app launch, swap it to a link that offers the app
  download.
- Turn that ~179 followers into ~1,000+ by posting consistently and cross-promoting
  in-store (a small "Follow us @sandwichfactorytn" card at the register).

### Facebook — [/sandwichfactorymurfreesboro](https://www.facebook.com/sandwichfactorymurfreesboro/) — **worth it: yes**
- Your audience here skews older/local and is your strongest social base.
  **Cross-post everything from Instagram automatically.**
- Keep hours, phone, and the order/app link current. Reply to comments and
  recommendations — it's a trust signal Facebook surfaces.
- Post weekly specials; boost one good post a month with a tiny ($20–30) local-radius
  ad targeting Murfreesboro.

### TikTok — **worth it: only if a staffer wants to run it**
- People already make videos about you ([example](https://www.tiktok.com/@thehungrywonderland/video/6989716598944746758)).
  That's free proof the food is photogenic. If someone enjoys it, post 1–2 short
  videos/week (sandwich builds, "best Cuban in the Boro"). If not, **don't** open an
  account you can't feed — a dead account looks worse than none.

### X/Twitter — **worth it: no.** Skip.

### Cross-promoting the app + Stars (once live)
- Add **"Order pickup + earn Stars in the Sandwich Factory app"** to every bio,
  the website, Google, and the in-store register card / receipt.
- Run a launch post on Facebook + Instagram with a **download-only deal** (e.g.,
  "free side of fries on your first app order") — app-exclusive deals are already
  planned in the app.
- Seed it: your ~2,000 existing Orda customers are the launch audience for the new
  app + loyalty migration (Phase 4 in the plan).
